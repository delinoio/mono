Show the apps in Korean, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Spanish, French, German and Brazilian Portuguese in addition to English. Delino Site and Delino Docs serve each language under `/<lang>/` and keep English at the existing routes. Delino Accounts, the OwnSand console and the SandRun console select the stored language or the best match for the browser languages, and show a language menu on every page.
