The console no longer offers "Try again" for a `not_found` or `invalid_argument` error, for example on an unknown device, sandbox or organization. The organization failure notice now always has a "Go to your organizations" link, beside "Try again" when the error is retryable. When the route organization is not in a complete organization list, the top bar switcher shows "Choose an organization" instead of the raw organization ID.
